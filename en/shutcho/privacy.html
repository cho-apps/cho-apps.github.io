shutcho Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the iPhone app "shutcho" (the "App") handles information. The App is provided by Chihiro Nakane.

1. Information the App handles

Trip names, purposes, and dates; plans (places, types, times, ways of travel, notes); gear lists; materials used and on-site work notes; expenses (category, item, amount, payment method, note) and receipt photos; links to the photos you add to trips; favorite places (name and location); trip members (name, company, role, phone number, email address, and note); the weather recorded for your trip days; the name, company, and department shown on your expense report; and settings such as appearance are stored on your iPhone. They are never sent to our servers. The App has no account sign-up.

You can also add a member with Choose from Contacts. The App then reads only the name, company, phone numbers, and email addresses of the one person you choose; it never reads your whole contacts list (and does not ask for access to your contacts). When you call or email a member, the App only passes the number or address to your iPhone's Phone or Mail app. Member details are other people's personal information; please enter only what you need for work.

If you turn on Notify Before Next Stop, the App schedules notifications on your iPhone with the names and times of your stops, so it can let you know a little before it's time to leave for the next one. The notifications are not sent to our servers.

2. iCloud backup

When "Back Up to iCloud" is on (it is on at first), this data, including receipt photos, is also saved in your iCloud (your private area in Apple's CloudKit) and kept the same across iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. We cannot see this data. If you turn it off in Settings, data is stored only on your iPhone from then on.

3. Photos and camera

The App reads your photo library to find photos taken on your trip dates so you can add them to a trip.

  • Photos are not copied or sent anywhere on their own. The App saves only which photos belong to which trip (the photo identifier and the time it was taken).
  • Only when you choose Export All does the App copy the photos you select into one file (zip). Location information inside the photos is removed. You choose where to send or save it.
  • You can scan a receipt with the camera or choose one from your photo library. Receipt photos are saved only inside the App (and in your iCloud if iCloud backup is on). They are not saved to your photo library.
  • The camera is used only to scan receipts.
  • The amount, date, and store name are read from the receipt on your iPhone. Receipt images and the text read from them are never sent anywhere.
  • You can change or turn off photo and camera access at any time in iPhone Settings. The other features still work.

4. Things you export

Expense reports (Excel and PDF), plan PDFs, work report PDFs, and photo files (zip) are made only when you choose to export them. An expense report includes the name, company, department, trip details, and expenses you entered (and receipt photos, if you choose). A work report PDF includes the name, company, and department you entered, your work notes, and the materials used (and work photos, if you choose). A plan PDF or work report PDF includes members' names, companies, and roles only if you turn on Include Members (off at first), and their phone numbers and email addresses only if you also choose Include Contact Details.

A trip file (.shutcho) made with Send Trip includes the trip's name, purpose, dates, plans, and gear. If you turn on Include Members (off at first), it also includes members' names, companies, and roles, and their phone numbers and email addresses only if you choose Include Contact Details. A file you send stays with the recipient and in the history of the app you used to send it (such as a messaging or email app). It never includes expenses, receipts, photos, materials used, work notes, the name, company, and department on your expense report, or any information about your device or account. A trip file you receive is read only when you choose to import it, and nothing is sent anywhere. You choose where to send or save them; they are never sent to the App's provider.

5. Maps, place search, and weather

The App uses Apple's maps (MapKit) and weather (WeatherKit) services to search for places, calculate travel times, find places nearby, and show the weather. Your search words and the places in your plan are sent to Apple for this. Apple handles this information under its privacy policy. The App uses your current location only if you allow it, and only while you are using the App: to plan from where you are, and to compare it with your plan on the day of your trip and adjust the times. Your location is not recorded or sent to our servers. When you search for places or calculate travel times from where you are, your location is sent to Apple.

  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

6. Ads

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC.

  • The App does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency). Ads are not personalized to your interests.
  • To show ads, Google may collect device and app information (such as IP address, device type, OS version, and ad views and taps). Google handles this information under its own policies.
    • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • If you buy "Remove Ads," ads will no longer be shown. Expense reports, plan PDFs, and exported photos never include ads.

7. Purchases

The "Remove Ads" purchase and payment are handled by Apple's App Store. Neither the App nor we receive your credit card or other payment details. The App only checks with Apple whether the purchase has been made.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Deleting your data

Deleting a trip in the App removes its plan, gear list, expenses, receipt photos, and photo links (photos in the Photos app stay). Deleting the App removes the data from your iPhone. To delete data saved in iCloud, go to iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. We do not hold any copy of your data.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
