FAQ

Can I move my data to a new iPhone?
If Settings → Back Up to iCloud is on, your data (including receipt photos) moves to a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. Work photos show on the new iPhone if iCloud Photos is on.

How do I get exported photos onto my work Windows PC?
Save the file (zip) made with Export All to Files and copy it to a USB drive, upload it to OneDrive or Google Drive, or send it by email. When you open the zip on your PC, you'll find the photos (JPEG) in folders by date. If you send them by email, choose Smaller for the photo size.

Can the expense report match my company's form?
The App uses one report layout. If your company has its own form, copy the details from the exported Excel file.

My trip photos aren't gathered automatically.
If photo access is set to "Limited" or "None," the App can't gather them automatically. Go to iPhone Settings → shutcho → Photos and choose "Full Access."

Travel time isn't filled in.
Walking, driving, and train times come from Apple Maps automatically (trains only where Apple Maps has transit directions). For flights, or when a time doesn't come in, enter it yourself. Stops entered by name only don't get travel times automatically.

I bought "Remove Ads," but I still see ads.
Go to Settings → Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.
