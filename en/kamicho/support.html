FAQ

If I delete a style, are the photos deleted too?
No. The style is only removed from the App; the photos stay in your photo library. The same goes for removing a single photo from a style.

I deleted a photo in the Photos app, and it disappeared from the App.
The App shows photos directly from your photo library, so photos deleted in the Photos app no longer appear in the App. If you restore them from Recently Deleted in the Photos app, they'll show up again.

I want to undo edits I made in the App.
Tap "Revert to original" in the App's edit screen, or open the photo in the Photos app and tap Edit → Revert.

Photos don't appear, or I can't take photos.
Go to iPhone Settings → kamicho and check that Photos and Camera are allowed.

The photos I want don't appear when choosing photos.
With Limited Access, only photos you've shared with the App appear. Tap "Show more photos to kamicho" on the choosing screen to add more.

Can I move my data to a new iPhone?
Not in the current version; styles and tags can't be moved. Your photos are in your photo library, so you can move them with iCloud Photos or similar.

I bought "Remove ads," but I still see ads.
Go to Settings → Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.
