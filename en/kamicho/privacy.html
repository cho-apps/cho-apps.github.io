kamicho Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the iPhone app "kamicho" (the "App") handles information. The App is provided by Chihiro Nakane.

1. Information the App handles

The App stores your styles (when they were taken and which photos they include), the tags you add (length, color, style, men's/women's), and settings such as appearance only on your iPhone. The photos themselves are in your iPhone's photo library. The App only keeps an identifier for each photo so it can tell which photo is which.

This information is never sent to our servers. The App has no account sign-up and no fields for client names or similar details.

2. Camera

The App uses the camera to take hairstyle photos. Photos you take are saved to your photo library. The App does not use your location and does not add location to the photos it takes. You can turn off camera access at any time in iPhone Settings.

3. Photo library

The App uses your photo library to:

  • Save the photos you take to your photo library and show them in the App
  • Let you choose photos you took before and add them to a style
  • Save your edits back to a photo when you edit it in the App (brightness, crop, background blur). iOS asks for your permission each time. You can undo the edits with "Revert" in the Photos app

Photos are never sent anywhere. Recognizing the person and hair for background blur also happens only on your iPhone.

If you choose "Limited Access," the App only handles photos it took and photos you selected. You can change photo library access at any time in iPhone Settings. How your photo library syncs (such as iCloud Photos) depends on your iPhone settings; the App is not involved.

4. Ads

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads are never shown on the screen for showing photos to clients.

  • The App does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency). Ads are not personalized to your interests.
  • To show ads, Google may collect device and app information (such as IP address, device type, OS version, and ad views and taps). Google handles this information under its own policies.
    • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • The App does not give Google your photos or tags for ads.
  • If you buy "Remove ads," ads will no longer be shown.

5. Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase and payment are handled by Apple's App Store. Neither the App nor we receive your credit card or other payment details. The App only checks with Apple whether the purchase has been made.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Deleting your data

Delete styles in the App, or delete the App, to remove styles and tags from your iPhone. Photos stay in your photo library; delete them in the Photos app if you want to. We do not hold any copy of your data.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
