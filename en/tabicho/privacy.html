tabicho Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the iPhone app "tabicho" (the "App") handles information. The App is provided by Chihiro Nakane.

1. Information the App handles

Trip names, dates, number of people, cover photos, tags (name and color), plans (places, times, ways of travel, costs, notes), packing lists, links to the photos you add to trips, places you want to visit (name, location, and note), favorite places (name and location), the weather recorded for your trip days, and settings such as appearance are stored on your iPhone. They are never sent to our servers. The App has no account sign-up. If you send a trip as a tabicho file, the people you choose receive its name, dates, number of people, cover photo, tag, plans, and packing list (you choose whether to include costs and notes). Photos added to the trip are not included.

If you turn on Remind Me Before the Next Stop, the App schedules notifications on your iPhone with the names and times of your stops, so it can let you know a little before it's time to leave for the next one. The notifications are not sent to our servers.

2. iCloud backup

When "Back Up to iCloud" is on (it is on at first), this data is also saved in your iCloud (your private area in Apple's CloudKit) and kept the same across iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. We cannot see this data. If you turn it off in Settings, data is stored only on your iPhone from then on.

3. Photos

The App reads your photo library to find photos taken on your trip dates so you can add them to a trip.

  • Photos are not copied or sent anywhere on their own. The App saves only which photos belong to which trip (the photo identifier and the time it was taken).
  • The PDF or image of your plan includes the cover and the photos you choose (up to 3). You choose where to send it.
  • Only when you choose Save to Photos does the App add the image of your plan to your photo library (using only permission to add photos).
  • Only the trip cover — the image you choose and crop — is saved inside the App (and in your iCloud if iCloud backup is on).
  • You can change or turn off photo access at any time in iPhone Settings. The other features still work.

4. Maps, place search, and weather

The App uses Apple's maps (MapKit) and weather (WeatherKit) services to search for places, calculate travel times, find places nearby, and show the weather. Your search words and the places in your plan are sent to Apple for this. Apple handles this information under its privacy policy. The App uses your current location only if you allow it: to plan from where you are, to compare it with your plan on the day of your trip, and to let you know when you are near a place you want to visit (only if you turn this on in Settings). Your location is not recorded or sent to our servers. When you search for places or calculate travel times from where you are, your location is sent to Apple.

  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Ads

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC.

  • The App does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency). Ads are not personalized to your interests.
  • To show ads, Google may collect device and app information (such as IP address, device type, OS version, and ad views and taps). Google handles this information under its own policies.
    • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • If you buy "Remove Ads," ads will no longer be shown. PDFs never include ads.

6. Purchases

The "Remove Ads" purchase and payment are handled by Apple's App Store. Neither the App nor we receive your credit card or other payment details. The App only checks with Apple whether the purchase has been made.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Deleting your data

Deleting a trip in the App removes its plan, packing list, photo links, and cover (photos in the Photos app stay). Deleting the App removes the data from your iPhone. To delete data saved in iCloud, go to iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. We do not hold any copy of your data.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
