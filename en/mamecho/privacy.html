mamecho Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the iPhone app "mamecho" (the "App") handles information. The App is provided by Chihiro Nakane.

1. Information the App handles

Bean names, origins, shops, notes, and photos; your cup logs (brew settings, place names, altitude, temperature, taste, score, notes, and photos); gear names and photos; and settings such as appearance are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to our servers. The App has no account sign-up.

2. Camera and photos

The App uses the camera to take photos of your beans, coffee, and gear. Photos you take, or choose from your Photos library, are saved only on your iPhone as photos for that bean, cup, or gear and are never sent anywhere. When you choose photos, the App reads only the photos you pick. You can turn off camera access at any time in iPhone Settings.

3. Sharing as PDF or image

When you share a cup or a comparison as a PDF or image, the App creates it on your iPhone and hands it to iPhone's sharing options (such as AirDrop or Messages). You choose where it goes. If you choose "Save to Photos," the App adds that image to your Photos library (it only asks for permission to add photos and does not read your other photos). Neither the App nor we receive the PDF or image.

4. Ads

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC.

  • The App does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency). Ads are not personalized to your interests.
  • To show ads, Google may collect device and app information (such as IP address, device type, OS version, and ad views and taps). Google handles this information under its own policies.
    • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • The App does not give Google the beans or cups you log for ads.
  • If you buy "Remove ads," ads will no longer be shown.

5. Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase and payment are handled by Apple's App Store. Neither the App nor we receive your credit card or other payment details. The App only checks with Apple whether the purchase has been made.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Deleting your data

Delete cups, beans, or gear in the App, or delete the App, to remove the data from your iPhone. We do not hold any copy of your data.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
