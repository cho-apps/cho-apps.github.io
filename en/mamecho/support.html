FAQ

Can I move my data to a new iPhone?
Not in the current version. Your data is stored only on your iPhone.

I bought "Remove ads," but I still see ads.
Go to Settings → Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Are altitude and temperature filled in automatically?
Not in the current version. Tap altitude or temperature when you log a cup to enter them by hand (you can leave them blank). The App does not use your location.

When does the comparison highlight a row?
Rows are highlighted when the two cups of the same bean have different values. For altitude, a row is highlighted only when the difference is 50 m or more, so GPS jitter doesn't count as a difference.

I want to see °F and feet.
Go to Settings → Units to switch between °C · m and °F · ft. Temperature and altitude change together.
